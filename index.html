Controls

Window 3 transfers take at least 3 seconds off-screen. One car can wait off-screen behind the car at Window 3; further requests queue at Present. The next car appears 1 second after Window 3 clears, once its transfer is complete. Results are recorded on leaving Present; the waiting timer starts on arrival.

Window 3 transfer space is clear.

Keyboard shortcuts: 1 = Add Lane 1, 2 = Add Lane 2, Q = Order 1 complete, W = Order 2 complete, C = Cash complete, P = Present complete, H = Send to Window 3, R = Window 3 complete, F = Fullscreen.
